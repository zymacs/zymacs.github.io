If you look around my blog you will see a lot of articles. Not so many but year, many in some sense.

The problem.

Many topics. Its hard to tell what I am into. If you asked me, I would't really have a ready made answer. Actually, now that I think of what answer I'd have for the question, one comes to mind: I want it all.

But then I have a "Hire Me" button in the footer. "Hire Me" for…

I guess what am trying to say is I'm making a bit of a change. Or, let's say, attempting to. I do have a lot of interests and IT is wide. All topics seem fun to dive into. But, without a plan, without a goal to run towards, without some solid signal this is who I am on this site, this won't be of any gain to anyone. To me.

So a rough plan. I had uploaded a list of books I was reading. I will cut that down to two. Git and CPP.

I will also be making sure to not write about anything not related to what my primary reading covers. I do alot of reading but I guess I should be selective about what makes it to the site. Most of it can remain as personal notes and come to the site when there is purpose for it.

Say I have a lot of drafts on memory, ram, cpus. I can dump all these out when I set out on a project that needs them all for prerequisites. I guess that'll be easier on my brain than what I have now. It's pulling away at the brain from multiple directions.

So recap

  • CPP
  • Zhou Finance
  • Last Week In Code

This should last at least as long as .. Let's try one week.

The sacrifice that is necessary. - Killing my so many random reading pursuits
