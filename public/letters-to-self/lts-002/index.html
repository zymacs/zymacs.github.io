It's going to hurt, like it should

Even if it will soon be over like the platitude states, it is going to hurt. Yes it will. You can optimize and hack around all you want to, but for some of what you want to achieve, there's no way to the goal but through the fire.

And don't complain about it like it's a bad thing. It's by design.

The reward would not be anywhere close to as valuable as it is, if getting it did not involve some hurdles.

Bite onto something. This isn't the pain that kills. This is the pain that lasts only for a while and , makes the victim better.

You want the reward, pay in endurance.
