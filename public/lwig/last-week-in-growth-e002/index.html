Last Week In Growth E002

June 18, 2026

So last week I posted the first episode in the series and made some ambitious promises. I did not keep my word for most of them. But, that's why I have this blog. I like that I have the evidence for my progress or lack thereof now publically available. Let's see if next week can be better.

I've resolved to give this blog a similar structure as /lwic. I'll be writing some updates from me. Then in the last section linking to some articles I read for self growth and productivity. The separation is becoming a bit of a problem. It seems there's noticeable overlap between /lwic and /lwig. But I'm hoping differentiation will arise as I write more uploads.

This first one will be me listing all areas that I think need to be worked on. I have no plan yet. Just a list of what needs work. Over the next series a plan and means to track progress will emerge I am hoping.

Updates from me

Time management

The movies problem

I was writing a few days back about how short time is. Yet how easy it is, when wanting to waste it, to have a skewed estimate for how much for it is left. I spend around 2.5 hours a day on movies every day. Sometimes when I get hooked this can go as high as 8 hours. You can see there's obviously an issue. My self control is not so good. Watch that I ain't saying watching movies is bad. What I'm saying is any activity that's taking more time than it should is not good. This for me is more time than I can afford to allot to movies.

And I think replacing it and anything else with alternative leisure that fast paces me towards my goals is something that I should easily do. It is possible for one to change their taste palates for what's entertaining if they put in the work. But I have for all my attempts at doing that kept on failing. Or, bailing out before my taste palates changed. Well, I haven't completely given up. I am trying again. Maybe differently this time.

I have tried reading for a replacement before. But it usually is the case around movie watching time my brain can't take on reading tasks. So I just shut down on paragraph one. Then go through the frustrating task of figuring out at what point in the book that happened come next reading session. The other replacement was leisurely programming. But this only worked when I had a comfortable working environment. Maybe it's a different reason for why its hard now. But I'll let that wait for when I improve my working environment.

I think the only option I have left now is sleeping early. Not doing a thing past 11:15 pm. Taking that as an op to wake up early. It's going to be quite a busy week this coming one, with prep to relocate but I think that won't affect my sleep schedule or planned schedule much. I can start on the sleep at 11:15 pm plan this week. Let's see how it goes. Sleep at 11:15, wake up at 5:45 in the morning.

How about a release? I shall set aside Friday night for a single movie. I will be taking an off from shows for a while then. My goals need a level of sacrifice and I guess watching shows is one of those things I will lay up as that.

Financial health

Another area that needs work. I can't write all that I need to change about my financial life in one paragraph so I'll go one problem at a time. Maybe every now and then introduce another. So I'd like to start with awareness. Keeping log for what is spent. What other means to be conscious of waste than to have a track record for where it all goes. I have a cli app to help with that already. So the problem is using the app.

I was thinking about why I would find such a simple but desirable task as logging my own expenses would turn out to be so hard. But I guess that does not help make it any easier so I'll stick to thought that helps. Well, I have realized that for most of what seems hard that actually isn't, after consistently forcing oneself to do it, it eventually becomes easy. But takes time.

I was also thinking of starting a public fin log. I have seen some do it. I probably only have 30 bucs in my account but wouldn't it be fun to see that explode.. In I don't know how many years. But that's for later.

Physical health

Workouts. I have plans, have had plans for a long time to get into calisthenics and martial arts. But before I stick to a basic pushups regimen, that will for ever be a pipe dream. I am restarting that challenge this week. More about physical health in the next updates. I am thinking some of these sections could gain from having their own dedicated sections for the sheer volume of how much I have to write on them.

Core vision alignment

Is what I am doing bringing me closer to my current core goals? I a while a go added a /now section to the blog to help align all or most of what I did towards my current major goals. But did that help? No. Why did it not.

I mean, it did. I thought more about the major goals than I usually do. So that's a plus. But I had no actionable plan for what I was going to have to do daily to achieve those goals. I therefore kept doing whatever it is I was doing. Productive work that did not get me what I wanted long term but.. felt productive.

Also, I think I don't know what I want to become. I am not sure if my /now defined well enough if at all. Do I want to become anything? Or to make money? As in, am i really interested in becoming a Systems Engineer or in making enough money to push me out of the rat race. Fastest most efficient way, top way.

If you have to choose, and this is an important decision to make: Is it getting huge sums of money and checking out so as to do what you are really interested in or.. becoming "someone" in your slow growing profession that you are interested in.

Speaking of financial freedom over profession. I was looking at Marc Lou's blog a few minutes ago. Indie dev is what I think he does. I rememeber looking into the same after I just left school and making up my mind to learn app development just for that. Where that goal fell I don't rememeber.

Problem? Consistency. How about I create a list of items to stick to. Commit to a sinking ship just so to rip the gains from being consistent. I've created a section that ain't public called 'next_up' with what I will do next after what's on the 'doing now' section of the /now page is cleared.

Mental health

Mental health. What does that mean? What makes for good mental health. Here's what I think. Yeah, first what I think then in later episodes, I will read more and share info from external sources. Following one's plan. The perception of progress. A good night's sleep. Good feeding. Excercise. Socializing. Hobbies. Good feeding. All contribute a better mental state.
