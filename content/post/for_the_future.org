Choosing between Feyman and Dense note taking?

I use both. When I am sure I have time on my hands (but not enough
time to really think through things), I write relatively detailed
notes. It is time taking but I gain more than I would have from
trying to explain to myself on a low energy brain.

When I later have the energy, I create articles that are essentially
me, explaining to myself the concepts from my notes.


What gains have I here?
- When I am down, I can still make progress covering material
  I am sure I will need for some future work.
- When my own explanations are lacking in anyway, I don't have to
  go back to the source material (unless it is lacking), only
  to my detailed notes for reference.
- The Feynman notes (or explanations to self) can take a single
  paragraph from the detailed notes and expand it to as many
  as my explanation needs to span. I can create cheatsheets
  from the detailed notes and understand what I feel I don't
  from the explanations about those notes. (all of which the system
  makes it so I have written)
- Also, since the detailed note taking work takes quite a load of time
  even when its not as draining as trying to explain things to self,
  It serves as long term savings. Say I got some project in the future
  that needs for me to know how to use some tool or know X about
  some language. I can go through all primary sources and make
  quick notes for a lack of time then get back to whatever else
  has me occupied. When usage time is around the corner, I can
  come back to my notes and explain to self from them.
