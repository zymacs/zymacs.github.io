This is the first post in this series.
I have no idea if its the last. It could be.
My past experience doing such kinds of
challenges says it possibly is. But, who am I to
pull the plug on what could be.

Had the same sentinment for  [[/lwic/last-week-in-code-001][lwic]] and [[/lwic/last-week-in-code-008][8 weeks]] later,
it still stands.

/May the force be with it./

So in this series of posts, a weekly series,
I will be sharing updates on progress
getting better in a variety of life domains. Could be social,
spiritual, related to career,
intellect, any problem I have no issue making
a public record of.


Why share? That's a valid question. I have for long
struggled with pushing myself forward while no one was watching.
Some do so. And great for them. I tried accountability
partners but it was hard to find anyone that had the same goals
as I did. I would say its impossible.

I wouldn't try coaches
since I ain't looking for a life coach. This? this is
public yet not public. I have the impression am creating
a public record while simultaneously aware there's probably
no one watching. So there's pressure to turn up and do things at the pace I set.
Yet not enough to make me quit out of 'PRESSURE TO PERFORM'.

Now I ain't yet certain on what structure its going
to take but I ain't got the time to wait for when I will.
So I'll start out rough and let the structure naturally
emerge.

A list of areas from which I will be sourcing challenges to work on.
- Health related
- Getting my dream job
- Getting a masters scholarship
- Productivity and time management
- Finance
- ...
  
  
I am thinking of creating a separate page as an index for all these
categories. Each link leading to a collection of updates for that
challenge. Then a weekly post that's me summarizing my progress
in all active challenges.

I was thinking I could probably have a separate update post
for each challenge but figured I'd crush under the pressure
to write all those posts for each.

I'll start simple. I have a movie watching habit. I need
to go 14 days using the time I was spending on movies for
adding to one of my projects. The link to the project details
is [[/challenge_records/no-movies/][here]].

Alright, that's it for this one.

/Wish me luck./
