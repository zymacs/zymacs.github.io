It seems  there's things which after you learn they are dangerous, you
grow to avoid them for ever. Fire burns. You only have to get burnt
once. The memory will be for ever effective. However, for certain things,
even after multiple learning lessons, we keep returning to put our
hands in the proverbial fire.

AI. AI makes mistakes. The chat clients consistently flash that
'information might be wrong' warning. But I think its useless.  And
I think, for how much money these companies have, they can afford the brains
to tell it is psycyologically upside down to hope that warning would be
as effective as a fire burn would in deterring one from fire.

After you send your first message, that warning is already forgotten.
These things respond with a kind of confidence that's hard to see
through. Even when sometimes, for topics about which we
are proficient, we catch them lying and tell ourselves we shall
forever be skeptical, a few minutes later, we are confidently
self diagonising with Bixonimania.

And trusting we really have it because the huge
AI company's chat bot would never lie. I mean, it
has read millions of books. It is very fluent. And
one can only be fluent if they know their stuff .. right???

I thought we just caught the fluent AI's lie a few minutes a go and
make up our minds we
would forever be skeptical..
LLMs, the asbestos of the information architecture.
LLM warnings are like warnings on cigarette boxes.
Catch my drift?

What solution then to this kind of tool. Which after you realise its
faulty, continuing to use it erases your memory about the realisation.
So you have to wait to realise again. And again.. And..

Avoidance.

I am very sure anyone that is not me. Since I am not popular at all
so only I know who I am, searched for Zymacs, they would leave
Gemini with a very warped impression. Zymacs is a mathematician
interested in algorithms and Computer Science. (with a few links
that since Gemini has already summarized them, its very unlikely
the reader will click them).. I mean, the reader has learnt to
just trust the first summary.. right.. but then.. the links
lead to random linked in profiles that aint Zymacs (aint me). The info from
which Gemini is building its case is two half written posts I did on
Probability. Yet its profile of me reads like a wikipedia
entry on Isaac Newton.

Its the thief with a toy arm in a supermarket..
convincing all there lives are on the line if they dont comply..

You have to know the gun is 100 percent a sham to call the bluff.
